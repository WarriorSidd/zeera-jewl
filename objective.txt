======================================================================
PROJECT OVERVIEW
======================================================================

We are building a complete Jewelry Manufacturing ERP.

This software will replace the client's existing software called JewelKAM.

The goal is NOT to clone JewelKAM.

The goal is to build a significantly better and more modern system while retaining the workflows that the client is already familiar with.

Initially, this software will be used by only one company.

It is NOT a SaaS product.

It will be hosted on our own Hostinger Cloud server.

We want to avoid paid third-party infrastructure as much as possible.

Everything should be self-hosted.

======================================================================
HOW THE BUSINESS CURRENTLY WORKS
======================================================================

Currently everything is managed manually.

When a customer places an order:

The office receives the order.

Someone manually creates a record.

The owner decides which karigar should manufacture it.

The owner contacts the karigar through WhatsApp or phone calls.

The owner sends images manually.

The owner explains specifications manually.

The karigar replies manually.

Pricing discussions happen over WhatsApp.

Delivery dates are discussed over WhatsApp.

Progress photos are sent over WhatsApp.

Final confirmation is also communicated manually.

All conversations become scattered across chats.

There is no central history.

There is no structured communication.

There is no audit trail.

There is no workflow management.

Finding previous orders is difficult.

Tracking production is difficult.

Managing hundreds of orders becomes difficult.

======================================================================
OUR VISION
======================================================================

We do NOT want another traditional ERP.

We want to combine the best ideas from

• Jira
• ERP Systems
• Manufacturing Workflow Software
• Project Management Tools

Every jewellery order should behave like a work ticket.

Instead of just creating an order, we want to manage its complete lifecycle.

Think of every order as a Jira Issue.

Every order has

Unique Ticket Number

Status

Priority

Timeline

Comments

Attachments

Assignee

History

Activity Log

Production Progress

Due Date

Discussions

Documents

Images

Every action should be recorded.

Nothing should happen outside the system.
