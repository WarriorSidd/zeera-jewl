FEATURE BACKLOG (PT-FIRST)
==========================

---
- Production Ticket lifecycle: creation, assignment, acceptance, stage transitions, QC, delivery, archive [L]
- PT Timeline & Immutable Audit [M]
- Assignment & Acceptance mobile flow for Karigars [M]

- PT-centric Dashboards (Owner, PM, Karigar) [M]
- Basic Invoicing linked to PTs [M]
